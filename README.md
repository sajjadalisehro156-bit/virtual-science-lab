# Virtual Science Lab

Govt. Boys Degree College Bhan Saeedabad

A web app that lets students perform science practicals on a phone or
computer, following the Sindh Textbook Board, Jamshoro syllabus for
Class 11 and 12.

## Features
- Physics, Chemistry and Biology practicals (Class 11 and 12)
- Computer Science, Arts & Commerce and Mathematics practicals
- Visit Our Lab: interactive lab map
- Play Demo in practicals, and a Lab Diary with instant feedback
- Quiz, Books, Question Papers, EduStories and EduSim 3D Studio
- Study Tools: Formula Calculator, Flashcards, Science Glossary,
  Study Planner and Lab Safety Checklist
- Works on mobile and laptop, with light and dark mode

## How to use
Open the live link, or download `index.html` and open it in any browser.
